# Concurrency is not Parallelism
[Great talk about concurrency](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cN_DpYBzKso) by a [famous Computer Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Pike).

I've been playing around with [Go](http://golang.org) on and off for years and always been intrigued by its built-in concurrency.  This talk is a masterful explanation of what concurrency really means, which really has nothing to do with the [Go language](http://golang.org) at all.  Don't miss this talk; it's not just for [gophers](http://blog.golang.org/gopher).
