# Language empowers thought

Take some time and read this[interview with Aaron Maxwell](http://www.blog.pythonlibrary.org/2015/07/27/pydev-of-the-week-aaron-maxwell/),
the author of the [Advanced Python Newsletter](http://migrateup.com).  The
interview revolves around [Python](http://python.org) because that's what Aaron
Maxwell specializes in.  However, there are some real gems here even for
non-Python developers.

> This matters, because language empowers thought. Code is not just a way to
> control a machine. It becomes a basic medium in which we express the mental
> creativity of our craft. A language can hinder or support that expression.
> A lot of it depends on making good decisions on details of the language.

That's why developers, whether they know it or not, identify with a specific
language and bicker over what language is better.
