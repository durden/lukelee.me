# Pycon 2013
I had the pleasure of attending [Pycon 2013](https://us.pycon.org/2013/) and [speaking](http://pyvideo.org/video/1751/building-full-stack-scientific-applications-in-py) last weekend.

The conference was excellent as always.  Maybe I'll have a more detailed post later about the experience, but in the mean time let's get to the good stuff.  Some of my favorites talks were the two by [Raymond Hettinger](https://twitter.com/raymondh).  Unfortunately, those are not posted online yet.  So I'll try to follow up later.

However, out of all the talks I saw the following really stood out AND are online already (in no particular order):

 - [Keynote by Eben Upton](http://pyvideo.org/video/1668/keynote-2)
 - [Python profiling](http://pyvideo.org/video/1770/python-profiling)
 - [How the Internet works](http://pyvideo.org/video/1677/how-the-internet-works)
 - [Python 3.3 trust me it's better than 2.7](http://pyvideo.org/video/1730/python-33-trust-me-its-better-than-27)
 - [Good enough is good enough](http://pyvideo.org/video/1738/good-enough-is-good-enough)
 - [How import works](http://pyvideo.org/video/1707/how-import-works)
 - [Server log analysis with Pandas](http://pyvideo.org/video/1745/server-log-analysis-with-pandas-0)
